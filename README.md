## Theme status

Validated with Shopify's official `@shopify/theme-check` v3.29:

```
ERROR: 0   WARNING: 6   INFO: 0
```

The six warnings are all `RemoteAsset` — Google Fonts rather than Shopify-hosted fonts. A deliberate trade-off, documented in `content/SETUP.md` under *Known trade-offs*.

**Not yet verified:** the theme has never been uploaded to a live Shopify store. It has been statically validated, not rendered. Step 9 of the setup guide is the click-through checklist for the first real run.

## Design

Single-theme light. Archivo (display) · Newsreader (body) · JetBrains Mono (data). Three colour settings drive every derived tone, so changing the accent restyles the store coherently.

No countdown timers, no fake stock counters, no invented reviews. The brand is positioned against unreliability, and every one of those signals unreliability — they are also now enforceable under the UK's DMCC Act 2024.
