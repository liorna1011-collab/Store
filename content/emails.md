## Flow 3 · Post-purchase — 3 emails

**Trigger:** order placed.

### 3A — immediately after the order confirmation

**Subject:** Here's how to set it up properly
**Preview:** Two minutes, and it decides whether this works.

> Thanks for the order — it goes out [today / tomorrow] and you'll get tracking.
>
> While you wait, the one thing worth knowing:
>
> **Seal balls straight after playing, while they're still warm.** Not the next morning. The leak is fastest in the first days after the can is opened, so every hour they spend loose in your bag is pressure you don't get back.
>
> Drop up to four in. Twist the cap. Three pumps. That's it.
>
> Any questions, just reply.

### 3B — 3 days after delivery

**Subject:** Start the 30-day test today
**Preview:** Photograph the gauge. It takes ten seconds.

> Your tube should have arrived. Here's how to hold us to our promise.
>
> Pump it to pressure and **photograph the gauge with today's date.** Do it again in 30 days.
>
> If it's dropped more than 1 PSI, email us both photos and we send a replacement — you keep the original.
>
> That's the whole guarantee. It only works because you can see the pressure, which is the reason the gauge is there.

### 3C — 14 days after delivery

**Subject:** Honest question
**Preview:** Did it work? Say so either way.

> Two weeks in. Has it made a difference?
>
> If yes, [leave a review] — it genuinely helps a new brand more than anything else we can do.
>
> If not, reply and tell me what happened. I'd rather fix it than have you quietly not come back.

## Setup notes

- **Sender name:** your actual first name, not "Sideglass Team". These emails are written as one person to another and the from-name has to match.
- **Plain text, or close to it.** No hero banners, no three-column layouts. The voice is a player explaining something, and a designed template undercuts that.
- **One link per email**, two at most.
- **Suppression:** never let someone be in Welcome and Post-purchase at the same time.
- **UK/EU law:** you need explicit consent for marketing email, a working unsubscribe in every send, and your trading address in the footer. Klaviyo handles the mechanics; the consent is on you — never import a list you did not collect.
