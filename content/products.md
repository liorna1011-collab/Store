## 1 · P4 Pressure Tube — the hero

**Title:** `P4 Pressure Tube`
**Handle:** `p4-pressure-tube`
**SEO title:** `P4 Pressure Tube — padel ball pressuriser with a gauge | Sideglass`
**SEO description:** `Padel balls lose 1–2 PSI a week. The P4 holds them at match pressure and puts a gauge on the cap, so you can check it instead of trusting it. 90-day pressure guarantee.`

### Description

Open a can of padel balls and the clock starts. They lose roughly one to two PSI a week from that moment — and a ball at 7 PSI pretending to be a ball at 11 does not tell you it has gone. You find out in the middle of a rally, when the bounce comes back dead and you start swinging harder to get the same ball back.

The P4 is a sealed aluminium chamber held above ball pressure. Balls inside it stop leaking, and balls that have gone soft slowly come back up. That part is not new — pressurisers have existed for years.

**What is new is that you can check it.**

Most tubes ask you to trust them. They leak slowly through a tired seal, and because there is no way to see inside, you carry on believing your balls are fine for months. A tube that leaks is worse than no tube at all: it gives you false confidence.

So we put the gauge on the cap. Open your bag, glance down, and you know. No app, no battery, no guessing.

**Ten seconds after every match**
Drop up to four balls in. Twist the cap. Three pumps. Into the bag.

**Why four balls, not three**
A standard can holds three. The fourth slot is for the spare that always ends up loose in the bottom of your bag — the one that dies first.

**What it will not do**
It will not resurrect balls that have been rolling around your boot since last season. Once the felt is gone, the felt is gone. The P4 is for keeping good balls good, and for bringing recently-soft ones back.

## 2 · Tack Overgrip — 12 pack

**Title:** `Tack Overgrip — 12 pack`
**Handle:** `tack-overgrip`
**Price:** £22.00
**SEO title:** `Tack Overgrip 12-pack — padel overgrips | Sideglass`
**SEO description:** `Tacky, absorbent padel overgrips in a 12-pack. One grip lasts 5–15 sessions, so a pack covers most of a season. Free UK delivery over £60.`

### Description

The grip your racket came with felt fine for about three weeks. Then it went shiny, and now your hand slides on every long rally — so you squeeze harder, which is how most people end up with a sore forearm blaming their technique.

An overgrip is the cheapest fix in the sport. This one is tacky when dry, still grippy when your hand is wet, and thin enough that it does not change the handle shape you are used to.

**One grip lasts 5 to 15 sessions**, depending how much you sweat and how hard you hold. Twelve in a pack is most of a season, or half a season and a few given away in the changing room.

**Fit:** padel and tennis handles. Comes with finishing tape.
**Thickness:** 0.6 mm.
**In the pack:** 12 grips.

## 3 · Edge Guard

**Title:** `Edge Guard`
**Handle:** `edge-guard`
**Price:** £12.00
**SEO title:** `Padel racket Edge Guard — frame protection tape | Sideglass`
**SEO description:** `Self-adhesive protection tape for the top edge of your padel racket. Takes the scuffs from the wall and the floor. Fits all standard frames.`

### Description

Everyone scrapes the wall eventually. Usually reaching for a ball you were never getting.

This is self-adhesive protective tape for the top edge of the frame — the part that meets the glass, the floor and the fence. It takes the scuffs so the carbon does not.

**Honest about what it does:** it stops scratches, scuffs and light knocks. It will not save your racket from a full-force collision with a wall. Nothing will.

It adds a few grams at the head, which control players sometimes notice on quick volleys. If you are sensitive to swing weight, that trade-off is real — it just costs a lot less than a new frame.

**Fits:** all standard padel frames.
**In the pack:** one pre-cut strip, alcohol wipe, applicator.

> Set this as your **post-purchase upsell** at £12. It is pure margin — the customer is already through checkout, so it costs you nothing to acquire.
