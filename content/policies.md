# Store policies — drafts

> ## ⚠️ Read this before using any of it
>
> **I am not a lawyer and these are not legal advice.** They are structured drafts that cover what UK and EU distance-selling rules generally expect, written so you are not starting from a blank page.
>
> Before publishing:
>
> 1. **Use Shopify's own generators first** — Settings → Policies → "Create from template". They are maintained against current law and prefilled with your store details. Paste these drafts *over* the parts you want in your own voice, rather than replacing the legal scaffolding wholesale.
> 2. **Have someone qualified read them** once you are taking real money. It costs a few hundred pounds and it is cheaper than one dispute.
> 3. **Fill in every `[bracket]`.** A policy with placeholders in it is worse than no policy.
>
> Areas that genuinely need a professional eye: VAT registration and the point at which you must register; IOSS for EU sales; UK GDPR and your data processors; and the DMCC Act 2024 rules on pricing, reviews and urgency claims, which came in with real penalties.

## Refund policy

**Your right to cancel.** Under the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations you may cancel within 14 days of receiving your order, for any reason. We extend this to **30 days**.

**Condition.** Items must be unused and returned in their original packaging. The Match Ready Kit may be returned with the overgrips opened — we do not ask for those back.

**How to return.** Email [support email] with your order number. We reply with a returns label. You do not need to give a reason.

**Return postage.** We pay for returns within the UK. For EU returns we pay where the item is faulty or not as described; otherwise the cost is yours.

**Refunds.** Issued to the original payment method within 5 working days of the return reaching us, including the original standard delivery charge.

**Faulty items.** Your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 are unaffected and run alongside anything above.

**The 90-day pressure guarantee** is separate and additional. See [Returns & guarantee](/pages/returns).

## Privacy policy

> Generate this one with Shopify's template rather than writing it. It must accurately list **your actual** data processors, and only you know which apps you installed.

It has to cover: what you collect (name, address, email, order history, and what your pixels collect); why; your lawful basis under UK GDPR; who you share it with — **name them**: Shopify, your payment processor, your courier, Klaviyo, Meta, TikTok, Google; how long you keep it; and the rights people have (access, correction, deletion, objection) plus how to exercise them.

**Cookie consent is not optional** for UK and EU visitors when you run Meta and TikTok pixels. Install a proper consent banner and connect it to Shopify's Customer Privacy API, or your pixels fire unlawfully.

## Claims you must be able to prove

The UK's Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 gave the CMA direct enforcement powers over exactly the things ecommerce stores get wrong. Every one of these appears somewhere in this store:

| Claim on the site | What you need before it goes live |
|---|---|
| "Loses less than 1 PSI in 30 days" | Your own 30-day test on production units, documented and dated |
| "16–18 PSI working pressure" | Supplier spec **plus** your own gauge reading |
| Any compare-at price | A genuine reference price — the real sum of the parts bought separately |
| "£20 tubes often hold 14 PSI or less" | Your own measurement of units you bought, described as a category not a named brand |
| Customer reviews | Real, from real customers. Fake reviews are now specifically illegal. |
| "Free UK delivery over £60" | An actual shipping rule configured in Shopify that matches |

**Fake urgency** — countdown timers that reset, "only 3 left" that is not stock-linked, "12 people viewing" — is squarely in scope. The theme ships without any of it, deliberately. Do not add it.
