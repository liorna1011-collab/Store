The theme renders an honest striped placeholder with a hint wherever an image is missing, instead of a grey box or a stock photo. That means the store is usable before you have photos — but it also means anyone can see what is missing, so do not run ads until this list is done.

You need **one good phone and one grey morning by a window.** Not a studio.

## How to shoot it

**Light.** One window, overcast day, subject side-on to the glass. A white sheet or A2 card on the shadow side. No flash, no lamps, never mixed light sources.

**Ground.** Light grey or off-white paper for product shots; a real court bench for context shots. Do not shoot on a wooden kitchen table — it reads as a hobby, not a brand.

**Camera.** A recent phone in its highest-quality mode. Lock focus and exposure by tapping and holding. Clean the lens. Wipe fingerprints off the tube between frames — anodised aluminium shows everything.

**Consistency beats quality.** Eight images at the same distance, same light and same angle look like a brand. Eight beautiful images at eight different angles look like a marketplace listing.

**Video: vertical 9:16, always.** Shoot more handle room than you think you need at the top — TikTok and Reels put UI over both ends.

---

## What not to do

- **No stock photos of padel.** Your buyer plays this sport and will recognise a Getty image of a tennis court instantly.
- **No AI-generated product images.** You are selling a physical object with a gauge reading on it. A generated image that implies a measurement you did not take is a false claim, not a design shortcut.
- **No images of a competitor's product** in your comparison section.
- **No photo of a gauge reading you have not actually achieved.** If your samples hold 15 PSI, the site says 15.

---

## Alt text

Every image needs it — for screen readers, and because Google reads it. Describe the object and what it is doing:

- ✅ `P4 Pressure Tube on a court bench, gauge reading 11 PSI`
- ❌ `product image 1`
- ❌ `padel padel ball pressuriser buy padel accessories UK`
